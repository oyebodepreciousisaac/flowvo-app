# Focused Discovery Questions

Use this as the minimum research checklist. Do not turn it into a broad research project.

1. Which small trade/service businesses have this workflow often enough for software to matter?
2. Where does a lead first arrive?
3. Who responds to it and how quickly?
4. How is a quote created and followed up?
5. What happens after a quote is accepted?
6. How is job completion recorded?
7. How is the invoice created and delivered?
8. What happens when payment is late?
9. Which follow-ups are manual today?
10. Which tools are already used?
11. What information gets duplicated between tools?
12. Where do missed handoffs or forgotten tasks occur?
13. What would make the current workflow materially easier?
14. Which parts must remain under human control?
15. What would make a buyer pay for this instead of keeping the current workaround?
