4. The 5-Stage Lead-to-Cash Operational Lifecycle

Flowvo replaces disjointed spreadsheets, paper receipts, and manual text messaging with an automated lifecycle chain where every stage naturally triggers the next:

Stage 01 Instant Capture
Inbound Lead Capture & Automatic Acknowledgment

Customer visits the business's public link (e.g. flowvo.app/enquiry/olalere-interior-decoration-d1jf), selects required services, and submits specifications. Within 400ms, Flowvo writes a new Lead record, fires a customer welcome email confirming receipt, and notifies the business owner on mobile.

Stage 02 Interactive Estimate
Interactive Quote Generation & Customer Dispatch

The business owner clicks "Create Quote" directly from the lead. The engine auto-populates client data. The owner adds itemized line items with unit pricing, labour costs, and VAT. Flowvo delivers an interactive quotation portal link via Resend. The customer can review, download, or accept the estimate on their mobile phone.

Stage 03 Automated Handoff
Quote Acceptance & Instant Job Scheduling

When the client taps "Accept Quote" and provides their digital signature, Flowvo triggers an atomic database transaction: quote status updates to ACCEPTED, and an active Job record is automatically generated with scope, timeline, and budget allocations preserved intact.

Stage 04 Field Execution
Job Execution & 1-Click Invoice Drafting

Technicians move the job from SCHEDULED to IN_PROGRESS to COMPLETED. When the technician marks the job complete, Flowvo instantly provisions a drafted Invoice, carrying over all agreed line items, client metadata, and tax figures. No manual retyping is ever required.

Stage 05 Settlement & Reputation
Multi-Currency Settlement & Google Review Trigger

The customer receives the invoice and pays through Paystack (NGN bank transfer or card) or Stripe (global cards, Apple Pay). Upon settlement, webhooks update the invoice to PAID. The system automatically schedules a Google Review email request to capture social proof when customer delight is at its highest.

5. Dual Payment Rails: Paystack & Stripe Engine

5.1 Paystack Subaccount Split Settlement (NGN)

For African and Nigerian commerce, Flowvo implements direct bank account settlements via Paystack Subaccounts:

  • Real-Time NUBAN Resolution (/api/banks/resolve): Verifies account numbers against Nigerian commercial banks, fetching the exact account holder name to eliminate settlement errors.
  • Automatic Subaccount Provisioning (/api/business/subaccount): Creates a dedicated subaccount (ACCT_xxxxxx) linked to the merchant. Payouts clear directly to their local bank.
  • HMAC SHA-512 Verification: Every webhook payload from Paystack is verified against the master secret key before marking invoices paid.

5.2 Stripe Global Payment Rail (GBP, USD, EUR)

For cross-border commerce, Flowvo integrates Stripe Hosted Checkout Sessions supporting Apple Pay, Google Pay, and international cards:

  • Platform Default vs. BYOK Mode: Businesses can use the built-in platform processing keys or toggle the developer accordion to provide their own Stripe keys.
  • Live Connection Health Check: In-app testing button executes real-time balance queries to verify credentials.

6. Autonomous Background Cron Daemons

Flowvo replaces manual human administrative chasing with three scheduled background daemons:

Daemon Route Trigger Frequency Autonomous Operational Logic
/api/cron/quote-followup Daily @ 09:00 UTC Identifies quotes in SENT state older than 48 hours without customer response. Dispatches a professional follow-up email with quote link. Capped at 2 attempts to maintain sender reputation.
/api/cron/invoice-overdue Daily @ 10:00 UTC Scans for invoices where dueDate < NOW() and status is SENT. Transitions state to OVERDUE and issues an escalated payment reminder with payment links.
/api/cron/review-reminder Every 4 Hours Scans invoices settled in the last 24 hours. Fires a branded Google Review request email with a direct rating link to maximize social proof.

7. Enterprise Security, Cryptography & Compliance

Flowvo was engineered according to modern enterprise security and privacy standards:

1. Timing-Safe Cryptographic Signature Verification: Webhook signatures are verified using Node.js crypto.timingSafeEqual() to neutralize side-channel timing attacks.

2. AES-256-GCM Credential Encryption: Merchant-provided BYOK API keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM authenticated cipher blocks before being committed to the database.

3. Strict Content Security Policy (CSP): Next.js security headers enforce strict script execution, frame ancestors restrictions, and explicit origin allowlists.

4. Rate Limiting & Anti-Abuse: Public customer intake forms implement IP-based sliding window rate limiting and honeypot form fields to eliminate automated bot spam.
