# Product Brand Method

Use this reference when the product is moving from validated problem to identity.

## Product brand hierarchy

1. User/problem truth
2. Product value proposition
3. Market/category expectations
4. Differentiating behavior
5. Brand personality
6. Visual identity
7. UX/UI expression

Do not reverse this hierarchy. A visual system should express a product strategy rather than hide the
absence of one.

## Positioning template

For [target user]
who [recurring problem/job]
our product is a [category]
that [valuable outcome]
unlike [current alternative]
because [meaningful difference].

## Personality test

A good personality trait changes a design decision.

Weak: “modern.”
Useful: “calm under pressure,” because the product should reduce visual noise, emphasize status,
and make recovery obvious.

Weak: “premium.”
Useful: “precise and dependable,” because typography, data density, confirmation behavior and error
handling should communicate control rather than luxury decoration.
