# Product and SaaS

## MVP
The MVP is the smallest product capable of producing meaningful evidence about the core value hypothesis. Define target user, job, workflow, value moment, must-haves, non-goals, risks, success criteria, constraints and measurement.

## Prioritization
Use RICE, ICE, MoSCoW, impact/effort, or opportunity-solution thinking as reasoning aids, not fake precision.

## UX states
Design initial, loading, success, empty, validation error, server/network error, permission denied, expired session, partial completion, retry/recovery, confirmation, and disabled/unavailable states.

## SaaS funnel
Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Efficiency.
Possible measures: activation, time-to-value, churn, retention, MRR, ARR, ARPU, CAC, LTV, NRR, conversion, CAC payback, gross margin.

## Business model
Subscription, seat-based, usage-based, tiered, transaction, hybrid, paid pilot, service-assisted SaaS. Tie pricing to value, usage, buyers, cost-to-serve, and alternatives.

## Alternatives
Analyze competitors plus spreadsheets, manual work, messaging, email, internal tools, agencies/services, and doing nothing.

## Success evidence
Workflow completion, repeat usage, time/error improvements, retention, payment, referral, behavior change.
A polished demo is not equivalent to validated value.
