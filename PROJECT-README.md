# Lead-to-Cash Automation — Project Workspace

> Working title only. Do not treat this as the final product name.

## Working problem hypothesis

Small trade and service businesses may lose revenue and administrative time because lead response, qualification, quoting, job tracking, invoicing, payment follow-up, review requests, and customer reactivation can be fragmented across multiple tools and manual processes.

## Product hypothesis

Explore whether a focused, automation-first lead-to-cash workflow can help a small trade/service business move more consistently from enquiry to quote to job to invoice to payment, without forcing the business into an unnecessarily complex field-service platform.

This is a hypothesis, not a validated market conclusion.

## Source context

The starting signal is an Upwork project brief supplied by the user describing an Airtable + n8n system for:
- quote → job → invoice tracking,
- Google review requests after job completion,
- a simple job-status dashboard,
- instant lead follow-up,
- lead reactivation.

The project must be researched independently. Do not copy the client's implementation, proprietary workflow, branding, or private information.

## Product ambition

Build a genuinely usable SaaS product that can become:
- a live portfolio product,
- a GitHub case study,
- a social build-in-public story,
- and evidence for future Upwork work.

## Non-goals for the initial build

Do not begin by attempting to reproduce a complete Jobber/ServiceM8/Housecall Pro replacement.
Do not implement every possible automation.
Do not add AI unless it improves a defined workflow.
Do not spend weeks researching without a decision-relevant question.

## Success standard

The first usable release should complete one coherent lead-to-cash workflow end to end, have real persistence and business logic, handle important failure states, be deployed, and be understandable to a first-time user.
